Welcome!
I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Child Health and Development Institute (CHDI).
I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Delaware in August 2026. My research uses causal inference methods to study population health, mental health, and labor economics, focusing on policy impacts on communities.

Research interests: Applied Microeconomics: Health, Mental Health, Labor, and Development
